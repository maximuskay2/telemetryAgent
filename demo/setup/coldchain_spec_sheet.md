# COLDCHAIN CCR-400T - Specification Sheet

Same content as `coldchain_spec_sheet.pdf`, as Markdown. Upload this file
(or the PDF, or `coldchain_spec_sheet.txt`) on the Setup wizard's Teach
step to build the cold-chain profile.

## 1. STORAGE

Storage temperature range: 2-5 C. Keep food between 2 and 5 C.

Alarm: temperature above 8 C for 10 minutes or longer.

HARD LIMIT: temperature above 10 C at any time is critical.

## 2. DEFROST

Automatic defrost every 6 hours, lasting at most 20 minutes.

During defrost the cabinet may warm to 7 C. This is expected.

## 3. CLIMATE CLASS

Climate class T (tropical): approved for ambient up to 43 C.

In hot rooms the compressor runs harder; this is normal.

## 4. ELECTRICAL

Rated current: 1.2 A at 230 V. Refrigerant R600a.

Compressor inrush at start: up to 4x rated current for 3 seconds.

Inrush above 4x, or lasting longer than 3 seconds, is a fault.

## 5. DOOR

Restocking openings of up to 2 minutes are normal.

A door left open longer than 10 minutes is a fault.

## 6. FORBIDDEN

Never raise the setpoint above 5 C.

Never disable the temperature alarm.
